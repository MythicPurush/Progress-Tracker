# Progress Tracker - Life OS

A personal study progress tracker built to help you stay consistent, track study time by subject, visualize progress through calendars and heatmaps, and analyze weekly, monthly, and yearly performance.

## Features

### 📊 Comprehensive Analytics
- **Overview Dashboard**: Get a bird's eye view of your progress with key metrics displayed at a glance
- **Consistency Heatmap**: Visualize your study streaks and consistency patterns across the year
- **Study Patterns**: Identify your most productive hours and peak performance times
- **Performance Analytics**: Deep dive into weekly, monthly, and yearly statistics
- **Subject-wise Breakdown**: Track progress for each subject separately with detailed analytics

### ⏱️ Smart Timer
- **Subject-based Tracking**: Log study sessions for specific subjects
- **Accurate Time Measurement**: Wall-clock based timer that continues running accurately even if the app loses focus
- **Session History**: Review all past sessions with timestamps and duration
- **Quick Start**: Fast access to the timer from any page

### 📅 Calendar View
- **Visual Calendar**: Monthly calendar with color-coded study activity
- **Daily Session Details**: Click any day to see all sessions recorded that day
- **Easy Navigation**: Move between months with intuitive controls
- **At-a-Glance Progress**: See which days you studied and for how long

### 🎯 Consistency Tracking
- **Streak Counter**: Track your longest study streak and maintain consistency
- **Activity Distribution**: See when you study (by hour and day of week)
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identify your optimal study times and habits

### 🌱 Growth & Gamification
- **Level System**: Earn levels based on total study hours (1 level per 10 hours)
- **Growth Visualization**: Watch a digital tree grow as you progress through levels
- **Achievements**: Milestones at various study hour thresholds
- **Motivation Tracking**: Stay motivated with visual progress indicators

### 🔐 Data Management
- **Local Storage**: All data is saved locally on your computer
- **Backup & Restore**: Easy backup and restore functionality
- **No Cloud Required**: Complete privacy with local-only data storage
